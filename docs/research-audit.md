# Research audit, 20 October 2026

All **58 entries, 199 response rows and 107 distinct reference strings** were screened. Twelve cases form a **43-claim evidence pilot**. Screening, metadata checking and full-text claim verification are different activities. This review does not certify every publication, historical narrative or mapped coordinate.

Unsupported numerical performance claims and universal causal statements were replaced by conditional qualitative interpretations. Unsupported numerical geometry was removed from response labels. Whole-case evidence grades were removed. Several narratives were rewritten to avoid deterministic explanations of cultural practices. Original descriptions, matrices and bibliographies remain in `data/audit/original-cases-2026-10-20.json`; dispositions are in `data/case-audit.json`.

Every reference is recorded in `data/reference-audit.json`, with original/current citation, status, associated cases and any Crossref candidates. **A search candidate is not an accepted identity match or claim validation.** Books, sourcebooks, field records and unresolved publications remain pending. Failed searches are not evidence of non-existence.

## Implemented corrections

- Removed zone-wide future offsets and unsupported CMIP6 attribution, arbitrary analogue scores and outdoor counts derived from an indoor adaptive-comfort model.
- Introduced one versioned 1991–2020 baseline, explicit units and aggregation, and missing-safe summaries.
- Corrected selected bibliographic records against primary metadata, preserving verification URLs and original strings.
- Rewrote Cartuja Qanat around the contemporary Seville project; separated documented components from measured performance.
- Distinguished comparable-building evidence from mapped-site evidence.
- Displayed claim evidence and reference verification status in the case reader.

## Remaining verification

Complete pending primary-text checks before quantitative synthesis. Record page/figure locators, test configuration, outcomes, comparators and uncertainty. Correct metadata alone does not establish claim support.

Historical and social accounts, point specificity, geometry, taxonomy and image-to-site relationships need separate checks. Köppen codes remain editorial labels and have not been recomputed. Related type/example entries are retained pending an explicit corpus model, including Nalukettu, patios/riads and windcatchers. Purposive teaching selection cannot estimate worldwide prevalence or comparative effectiveness.
