# Methodology

This atlas treats vernacular architecture as climate-adaptation knowledge.

Rather than cataloguing forms only as heritage objects, the atlas interprets them through:

- the environmental pressures they respond to
- the physical mechanisms they use
- the transferable strategy families they represent
- the regional variants through which they become culturally and materially specific
- their limits, risks, and transferability

## Analytical sequence

```text
Climate pressure
↓
Architectural/body-scale response
↓
Microclimatic mechanism
↓
Transferability and limits
```

## Editorial principle

A case should not be added only because it is visually interesting. It should show a legible relationship between climate pressure and spatial, material, operational, or body-scale response.

## Evidence and research scope

Each response is assessed separately using the [claim evidence protocol](evidence-protocol.md). A documented design, measured effect, simulated outcome and editorial physical inference are distinct. Quantities require source locators, outcomes, comparators, conditions and limitations. No evidence grade is assigned from contributor origin.

The corpus is purposive and pedagogical. Case counts cannot establish worldwide prevalence or comparative effectiveness. Regional climate context does not establish indoor comfort or validate an architectural mechanism. See the [research audit](research-audit.md) for remaining source, history, location and typology checks, and [climate methods](data-method.md) for data provenance.
