# Case image coverage

Updated 20 October 2026. Added 56 reusable illustrations for 43 formerly unillustrated cases. Existing images for cases 25–33 are retained.

Photographs are selected for the visible construction, spatial arrangement, material or local use. Comparative regional examples and museum displays are explicitly labelled in captions. A photograph illustrates a feature; it is not evidence of a quantified climatic effect.

Sources, creators, licenses and links are visible below each new gallery image and recorded in `data/image-sources.json` and per-case `source.txt` files. The original image licenses apply to these files; they are not relicensed by the repository. Images are not cropped or retouched.
